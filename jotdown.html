Title and definition

JotDown: AI-Powered Smart Note App with Semantic (natural language) Search and Category Visualization (UX Design)

Why is it innovative?

JotDown brings together notes from different platforms onto a single application to make it easy to visualize and utilize previous notes.

How does it improve a person’s life?

Following the logic of the UX Heuristic Law #6, Recognition rather than Recall, JotDown utilizes note categories and visualization to let people recognize information in the interface, rather than forcing them to recall it.

What did I learn?

Leadership: I learned how to lead four junior designers, from creating a roadmap and mentoring design processes to leading their collaboration with developers.

Collaboration: I collaborated closely with developers, the technical team members. Instead of a single developer handoff at the end, it was a tightly braided process throughout.

Technical: Working with developers allowed me to better understand the latest technological capabilities and how engineering works with design.
