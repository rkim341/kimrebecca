The Project

Conversate builds conversational confidence and facilitates meaningful conversations for introverts.

The Problem:

Conversation cards like “We’re Not Really Strangers” are reciprocal, but start from zero every time and only happen among comfortable friends. Speech-training apps build skills over time, but users practice alone or with AI, so there is never reciprocation.

The Outcome:

An experience that combines real human reciprocity with skill-building that grows over time, specifically training conversation skills for social settings.

Why is it innovative?

Conversate is the only experience that combines real human reciprocity with skill-building that grows over time, specifically training conversation skills for social settings.

Existing conversation cards like “We’re Not Really Strangers” are reciprocal, but they start from zero every time and only occur amongst comfortable friends. Speech-training apps build skills over time, but users practice alone or with AI, so there is never reciprocation.

How does it improve a person’s life?

Users start with one minute of daily articulation practice by recording themselves, then move on to anonymous, low stakes practice with real people virtually. Finally, users can utilize deep prompt cards for in-person conversations.

Progress is easily visible and feedback compares users only to themselves, never to others, to encourage users to return and continue developing their conversational skills.

Learnings

I learned to be intentional about the usage of AI. It is useful for transcription and coaching, but when facing real interactions in replies, reflection, and real conversation, it is important to involve real human interaction.
